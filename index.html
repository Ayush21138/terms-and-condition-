Welcome to PartsBaba.ai

We believe sourcing electronic and hard-to-find parts should be simple, transparent and reliable. These Terms & Conditions are designed to clearly explain how our platform works and how we work with you to source the products you need.

01

Using PartsBaba.ai

PartsBaba.ai is a technology-enabled sourcing platform that helps individuals, engineers, businesses and organisations find and source electronic components, IT parts, hardware, accessories and other specialised products.

By accessing or using PartsBaba.ai, you agree to use the platform responsibly and in accordance with these Terms & Conditions.

Our goal is to make the sourcing experience easier by combining technology, supplier connections and human assistance.

02

How Our RFQ Process Works

Our Request for Quote (RFQ) process is designed to help you find the right product and understand its commercial details before placing an order.

01

Share Your Requirement

Tell us what part, component or product you are looking for.

02

We Source

Our team checks relevant supplier and sourcing options.

03

Receive a Quote

We share available pricing, specifications and expected lead time.

04

Confirm Your Order

Once terms are accepted and applicable advance is received, the order is confirmed.

Your RFQ is the beginning of the sourcing conversation.

Submitting an RFQ allows our team to understand your requirement and work on suitable sourcing options. It does not by itself create a confirmed order.

03

RFQ Responses

Once an RFQ is received, our team reviews the requirement and works with relevant suppliers to identify suitable options.

We generally aim to provide an update within 24–48 business hours, depending on the complexity of the requirement, supplier response time and product availability.

Some specialised, imported or hard-to-find products may require additional sourcing time. In such cases, we will keep you informed as the sourcing progresses.

04

Quotations & Pricing

Pricing shared through an RFQ is based on the information available from suppliers and other applicable commercial factors at the time of sourcing.

A quotation may take into account factors such as:

  • Supplier pricing and availability
  • Quantity requested
  • Currency exchange rates
  • Applicable taxes and duties
  • Shipping and freight costs
  • Product specifications and sourcing location
Clear pricing before confirmation

The final commercial terms will be shared with you before the order is confirmed. If supplier pricing or other sourcing conditions change before confirmation, we may need to update the quotation accordingly.

05

Product Availability

We work with our supplier network to identify available products based on your requirement.

Availability can vary depending on supplier inventory, market conditions, product lifecycle, location and quantity requirements.

Where a requested item is unavailable, we may help identify an alternative product, supplier or sourcing option where appropriate.

06

Lead Times

We provide an estimated delivery or sourcing timeline based on the latest information available from our suppliers and logistics partners.

Lead times can vary due to supplier processing, inventory movement, customs, transportation and other circumstances outside the direct control of PartsBaba.

If there is a significant change to an expected timeline, we will communicate the available information to you.

07

Order Confirmation

We want to make the distinction between an RFQ and a confirmed order clear and straightforward.

An order is considered confirmed when:

  • The quotation and applicable commercial terms have been accepted;
  • The required product and quantity have been confirmed; and
  • The applicable advance payment has been received.

Unless a different payment requirement is communicated for a particular order, PartsBaba may require an advance payment of 10% to confirm the order.

No surprise confirmation

We aim to make the confirmation stage clear. The applicable pricing, product details, payment terms and other relevant commercial information will be shared before the order is confirmed.

08

Payments

Payment requirements may vary depending on the product, supplier, order value and sourcing arrangement.

The applicable payment terms will be communicated as part of the quotation or order confirmation process.

Customers are responsible for providing accurate payment information and completing payments within the agreed timelines.

Any applicable taxes, duties, shipping charges or other commercial costs will be communicated as part of the relevant quotation or order.

09

Delivery & Shipping

PartsBaba works with logistics and supplier partners to arrange delivery according to the applicable order terms.

Delivery timelines may depend on product availability, sourcing location, shipping method, customs processes and destination.

Where tracking information is available, it may be shared with the customer to help monitor the shipment.

10

Returns, Replacements & Support

We want every sourcing experience to be as smooth as possible. If you receive a product with an issue, please contact our support team with the order details and relevant information.

Return, replacement or refund options depend on the product, supplier terms, product condition and circumstances of the request.

For products that are specially sourced, customised, imported or procured specifically against a confirmed requirement, applicable return conditions may differ.

Our team will review the situation and guide you through the available next steps.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and for activity carried out through your account.
